Philosophy

Why we're building this at all.

Some things are too tender for an audience.

Most of the internet was built to be watched. That changes what people are willing to say, and how honestly they say it.

Koemere starts from the opposite instinct. We build for the room, not the audience. The rest is still taking shape — but that standard isn't negotiable.
