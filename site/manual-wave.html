Chapter 04 • Early Cohort

Before there's an app, there's a handful of people.

The Manual Wave is our intentional, private first cohort. Founding members test early builds, talk directly with our creators, and define the core culture of Koemere.

  • Direct contact with the founders No support bots or automated tickets. You will be in a direct dialogue with the designers and engineers building the system.
  • Handcrafted early builds Early builds are intentionally unscaled. We test with small numbers specifically so we can adjust details with extreme care.
  • Direct influence on product decisions Your input directly dictates how room privacy thresholds, audio compression, transcripts, and moderation are shaped.
  • No spam, zero pressure to remain We respect your time. If Koemere is not for you, leaving is as simple and respectful as joining was.

Frequently Answered Questions

Everything you need to know about the Beta

Is participating in the Manual Wave free?

Yes, 100% free. Founder cohort members will receive lifetime early-supporter access and premium circle memory archiving at no cost.

What platforms will be supported?

We are starting with iOS TestFlight and an ultra-minimal progressive web client, followed by Android support during Phase 02.

How do you handle privacy & data?

Private group messages are end-to-end encrypted. We collect zero advertising identifiers, sell no user data, and track no external activity.

How are applications reviewed?

Every single application is read personally by our small founding team. We look for individuals and groups who genuinely value quiet, thoughtful connection.
