Chapter 02 • Architecture

Two spaces, for two different kinds of honesty.

Human beings don't have just one mode of communication. Sometimes we need the warmth of our closest confidants; other times we need a cathartic outlet without social consequences. Koemere provides a distinct home for each.

Space 01 • The Living Room

Group Space

An invite-only, private sanctuary designed for the 3 to 12 people who already know your history, your quirks, and your values.

In Group Space, voice messages are preserved inside an asynchronous pod. There are no timestamps to pressure instant replies, no blue checkmarks, and no public view counts. It is built around recurring relationships rather than ephemeral transactions.

  • Capped at 12 members per pod to ensure psychological safety
  • Asynchronous voice memos with clean, calm typography transcripts
  • Encrypted private memory archives for shared circle history
Private Pods Familiar Faces Zero Broadcasting
Space 02 • The Confessional

Public Venting Space

A calm, nocturnal outlet designed for the heavy thoughts, raw reflections, and unpolished confessions you cannot tell your immediate circle.

In Public Venting Space, posts are detached from your identity, disconnected from any persistent profile graph, and automatically sunset. You speak to the room, unburden your chest, and leave without digital residue.

  • Pseudonymous authoring with zero permanent profile linkage
  • Automatic graceful sunsetting after 24 hours
  • Empathetic, non-judgmental moderation without toxic comment pile-ons
Ephemeral Venting Cathartic Release Zero Profile Graph
Architectural Breakdown

How the Two Spaces Compare

Dimension Group Space Public Venting Space
Intended Audience Close friends, partners, family, or tight creative pods (3–12 people). The broader world in a calm, non-judgmental listening state.
Identity & Profile Recognizable identity within your closed pod. Detached pseudonymous handle with no public follower history.
Message Lifespan Archived securely for your circle to revisit memories. Gracefully sunsets and deletes automatically after 24 hours.
Audio & Text Format High-fidelity voice notes with gentle reading transcripts. Short reflective voice notes & measured text reflections.
Atmosphere & Vibe Warm, tender, familiar, and conversational. Nocturnal, cathartic, quiet, and unburdening.
Metrics & Feedback Direct intimate responses from people you love. Warm empathetic resonance notes (no public like counters).
